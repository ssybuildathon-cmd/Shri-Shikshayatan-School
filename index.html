ESCAPE: THE HOLLOW GALLERY

The doors sealed at 5:00 PM. Five puzzle stations stand between you and the way out, and each one only opens once you're holding the key the exhibit before it left behind. You have six ten-minute skips before 6:00 PM. Start at the Display Cabinet, then the Grandfather Clock, then the Numbered Dial Lock, then the Curator's Desk — solve each in turn to earn the key to the next, and finally The Vault itself. Above all: when something in this room starts watching you, look away.

How to play:
1. Walk up to any exhibit with a glowing gold beacon above it.
2. A prompt appears — press E or click to interact.
3. Solve the puzzle shown in the pop-up window (every puzzle has a free 💡 hint button).
4. Solving a puzzle hands you the key to the next one — a locked exhibit will tell you which key it needs.
5. Check the Objective box (top-right) any time you're unsure what to do next.
